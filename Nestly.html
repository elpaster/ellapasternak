Nestly

Making finding a compatible roommate easier, safer, and more reliable.

Overview

Nestly is an innovative mobile app designed to make finding a compatible roommate easier, safer, and more reliable. In a world where living with a stranger can feel like a gamble, Nestly provides users with a personalized matching experience based on lifestyle compatibility, shared interests, and mutual trust. The app goes beyond simple profiles by integrating fun and insightful quizzes that help users understand their own habits while matching them with others who share similar values and routines.

One of Nestly's standout features is its endorsement system, where users can leave reviews and recommendations for each other, building trust and creating a transparent and supportive community.

Target Audience College students, young professionals
Duration 12 weeks
Methods User interviews, empathy mapping, personas, wireframing, prototyping, usability testing
Role Lead UX Designer

The Problem

Living with another person is a significant commitment and many people struggle to find compatible and trustworthy roommates. Current roommate-matching platforms fail to offer reliable ways to assess compatibility beyond basic preferences, leaving users vulnerable to mismatches that can lead to conflicts, stress, and disrupted living situations. Users need a more effective way to find roommates they can build positive, lasting relationships with during the duration of their lease.

The Goal

Design an app that helps users find compatible and trustworthy roommates by combining personalized matching through quizzes with an endorsement system. The goal is to create a seamless, stress-free experience that fosters positive relationships and long-term compatibility.

Researching the problem space

Roommates are a crucial part of the housing equation for many people—nearly 30% of renters in the U.S. rely on them to make rent more affordable. But let's face it, finding a roommate you can actually live with is not as easy as it sounds. From awkward living situations to mismatched personalities, the search for the right roommate can be a stressful and overwhelming journey. That's why I set out to better understand the motivations, pain points, and preferences of people looking for roommates, so we can create a solution that feels like a breath of fresh air.

Research Goals

  • Understand who is searching for roommates and their motivations
  • Identify frustrations users face when searching for roommates or housing
  • Explore key features that would improve the roommate matching experience
  • Analyze current roommate apps and their strengths/weaknesses
  • Validate the need for trust-building features like reviews and endorsements
  • Investigate user preferences for roommate search filters (e.g., lifestyle, location, budget)

Key User Insights

Roommate selection is an emotional process

Finding the right roommate isn't just transactional, it's deeply personal. Users often feel a blend of excitement and anxiety, seeking both compatibility and emotional reassurance. The decision goes beyond finances and chores—trust and comfort play a major role in selecting a roommate.

Compatibility needs to be comprehensive

Roommates need to align on multiple dimensions—lifestyle preferences, personality, social habits, cleanliness standards, and financially. Finding someone with similar habits can reduce potential conflicts and create a more harmonious living situation.

The search process should be fast and intuitive

Users want a quick, efficient, and easy process when finding a roommate. A seamless interface with intuitive filters and simple matching tools saves time and eliminates frustration. The faster they can narrow down compatible candidates, the less likely they are to abandon the platform or feel overwhelmed.

Security comes before comfort

People need to feel safe—not just physically, but in the sense of knowing that their roommate is a legitimate person who can be trusted. Identity verification and safety features are not just "nice to have" for users, but essential for creating peace of mind, especially when living with someone they met online.

UI Design & Brand Identity

When exploring logo ideas, I was drawn to nature-inspired symbols that could represent community and shared living. I was tied between using a bee or a bird — both social, cooperative creatures — but after several drafts, I landed on a design featuring a blue bird perched outside a birdhouse. It felt friendly, calm, and symbolic of finding a place that feels like home. To support this tone, I chose a soft blue color scheme that feels approachable and trustworthy.

Live better, together.

Persona Development

Based on my user insights I developed two personas: Brie, a college student seeking a quiet, affordable living space, and James, a divorced father looking for a trustworthy, family-friendly roommate. These personas helped me understand the unique challenges and goals of my users.

Medium Fidelity Wireframes

To bring my ideas to life, I started by creating medium-fidelity wireframes. This helped me map out the app's core features, layout, and user flow without getting too caught up in visuals just yet. It was a way to explore how everything would function together.
