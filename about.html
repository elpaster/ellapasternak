My Philosophy

Accessibility

Accessibility is at the heart of my design process. I believe products should be inclusive and usable by people with a wide range of abilities, needs, and experiences. By identifying barriers and considering diverse needs from the start, I often uncover simple and thoughtful solutions that benefit everyone.

Collaboration

I'm a firm believer that collaboration sparks creativity. The best ideas often come from the intersection of different viewpoints and backgrounds. I thrive in environments where innovation is fueled by collective input. Open communication, shared brainstorming, and diverse perspectives always lead to stronger, more creative outcomes.
